## Starting point

This project in the Lower Birs Valley (Basel-Landschaft) involved the complete renovation of an older apartment kitchen. Converting from gas to electricity was part of the work. The supplied customer information describes a kitchen area of 25 m².

## More than new kitchen furniture

The described scope included a new electrical installation, water connections, plaster work and wall chasing. The renovation therefore also addressed the infrastructure behind the visible kitchen fittings.

The kitchen has two sections, with a length of 4.5 metres mentioned in the supplied information. Black stone is part of the design description. The design and technical work belonged to the same renovation project.

## Understanding the duration

The newer customer reference states 4–6 months. We describe this as the **total project duration**. It is not a commitment about the installation time of another kitchen project.

The cover photograph comes from the supplied customer package and shows the modernised kitchen. This article describes that particular project. No measured energy-consumption or savings figures have been supplied.
