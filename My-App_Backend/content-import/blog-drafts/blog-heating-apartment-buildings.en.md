## A project across two residential buildings

The supplied customer reference describes a heating modernisation in Allschwil (Basel-Landschaft). It covered two residential buildings with 18 apartments in total.

## Equipment and heat distribution

According to the project description, the old heating systems were replaced with heat pumps, thermal storage and distribution manifolds. Heat meters were retrofitted in every apartment. The customer information associates this with consumption-based billing.

Calculating the heat demand for hot water was another stated part of the work. This describes the scope of this particular project and is not a sizing recommendation for other buildings.

## Coordination with the management

For a project involving several apartments, coordination with the management forms part of the work. The supplied information explicitly mentions coordination with the condominium owners’ association management.

## Total project duration

The newer customer reference states 4–8 months. We describe this as the **total project duration**. It does not state how long heating was interrupted or establish a binding duration for another property.
